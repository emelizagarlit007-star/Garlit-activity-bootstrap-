What is the Cat Family?

About Felidae

The cat family, scientifically known as Felidae, is a group of carnivorous mammals. This family includes both domestic cats and wild species like lions, tigers, leopards, cheetahs, and jaguars.

All members of this family share common features: they have sharp claws, strong jaws, excellent eyesight and hearing, and are skilled hunters. They are found all over the world, except in Antarctica and some isolated islands.

Group Name: Felidae
Diet: Carnivores
Habitat: Global (except Antarctica)
Claws: Retractable (except Cheetah)

Fun Facts About Cats

🐾 Sharp Senses

Cats have a very strong sense of smell and hearing. They can hear sounds that humans cannot hear, which helps them hunt for food.

🐾 Claws

Most cats have retractable claws – this means they can pull them in when walking so they stay sharp, and pull them out when climbing or hunting.

🐾 Night Vision

Cats can see much better in the dark than humans. Their eyes have a special layer that reflects light, helping them see even when it is dim.

🐾 Purring

Both domestic cats and some wild cats can purr. They usually do this when they are happy or comfortable, but they also purr when they are sick or hurt.

🐾 Flexibility

Cats have very flexible bodies. They can twist and turn easily, which is why they are good at jumping and landing on their feet when they fall.
